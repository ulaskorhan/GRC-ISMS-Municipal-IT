Brattli IKT IKS: risks and controls

An inter-municipal IT company that runs IT for five municipalities, including health and care, social services and schools. This page shows the risk register and the Statement of Applicability (ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A). All data is read from the CSV files in this repository. Nothing is stored or sent anywhere.
